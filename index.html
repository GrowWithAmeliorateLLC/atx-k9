Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Everything Austin dog owners ask us most.
What dog training programs does ATX K9 offer?+
We offer board & train (1–4 weeks), private 1-on-1 obedience lessons, in-home puppy training for puppies under 16 weeks, and boarding for our training graduates — all serving Austin and the surrounding areas.
Where is ATX K9 located and what areas do you serve?+
We're a family-run dog trainer based in Elgin, TX, about 25 minutes east of Austin, serving the greater Austin area including Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Westlake, and South Austin.
How long is the board & train program?+
Board & train runs one to four weeks depending on your dog's needs. Your dog lives and learns at our facility and comes home with real obedience and better manners, plus two private follow-up lessons with you.
What training methods do you use?+
We're balanced, holistic trainers — positive reinforcement balanced with clear structure, tailored to each dog, focused on their whole physical and mental wellbeing.
Do you work with all breeds and ages?+
Yes — all breeds and ages. Puppies under 16 weeks start with in-home puppy lessons; dogs over 16 weeks are eligible for any of our programs.
How do I get started?+
Book a free consultation — fill out the form at atxk9.com or call (512) 508-8959. We'll talk through what's going on with your dog and recommend the right program.
