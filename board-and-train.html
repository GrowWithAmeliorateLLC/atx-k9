Board & Train FAQ

The questions we hear most

What do I need to bring?
Bring your dog's own food so their stomach stays settled. You're welcome to send bedding and chew toys for the crate (no stuffed or sentimental items, please), though we can't guarantee they come back in the same condition.
Will you handle my dog's diet and medication?
Yes. We accommodate all diets, including raw (pre-package and freeze daily meals). A veterinary technician on staff dispenses medication daily and cares for diabetic and special-needs dogs.
Do I get updates during the stay?
Absolutely. You'll get "report cards" every three days through our client portal, each with a written update plus a photo or video. Sample report cards live on our Instagram.
Can I visit my dog?
Yes, you can schedule a visit. We just ask you to consider your dog's temperament, timid or anxious dogs can find it confusing to see you come and then leave, so we'll help you make the best plan.
Where does my dog stay?
On our 2.5-acre property with two buildings, our home and a custom, climate-controlled training building. Days are spent with the family, in the house, outdoors (always supervised), and in the indoor training space. At night, every dog sleeps in their own top-of-the-line crate, indoors, in a fully climate-controlled building (AC in summer, heat in winter), a real step up from the traditional indoor/outdoor kennel runs most facilities use. The building is monitored 24/7 for fire, flood, temperature, and power, with a backup generator.
What's your guarantee?
With board & train, we guarantee your dog will display knowledge of the commands in the chosen package when they return home. After that, you follow through (we teach you how) and we're here for questions. Our goal is your satisfaction and great care for every dog.
Board & train or private lessons?
Board & train takes the teaching off your plate, we train the dog, you reinforce at home, with faster, immersion-style results. Private lessons cost less and build a deep bond, but progress depends on your daily follow-through. Not sure? We'll help you decide on your consultation.
