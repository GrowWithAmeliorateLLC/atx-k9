Boarding, Done Differently

A familiar place, with people they already know.

Leaving town is a lot easier when your dog is somewhere they already know. Because we've trained them, we know your dog's commands, routine, and quirks, so boarding feels like a stay with family, not a stressful drop-off at a strange facility.

Days are spent with our family on our 2.5-acre property, with daily walks, play, and the same calm structure they learned in training. At night, every dog sleeps in their own crate, indoors, in a fully climate-controlled building (AC in summer, heat in winter), an upgrade from the traditional indoor/outdoor kennel runs most facilities use.

Just so you know: we offer boarding to dogs who've completed a training program with us. New here? Start with a program and boarding opens up afterward.
