Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

ATX K9 Dog Training ("ATX K9," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website and forms, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting a form, you agree to this Policy.

Information we collect

When you fill out a form or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Information about your dog (name, age, and what you'd like help with)
  • Appointment and consultation details you provide
  • Basic technical data, such as your browser type and pages viewed

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule your consultation
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders, and updates you've requested
  • To provide and improve our training services and customer support
  • To comply with our legal obligations

SMS / text-message program

If you opt in by checking a consent box on our form, you agree to receive text (SMS) messages from ATX K9 Dog Training at the phone number you provide. Two separate, optional consents are available:

  • Transactional: appointment confirmations and reminders related to your consultation.
  • Marketing: occasional updates, tips, and offers.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and reply HELP for help. Opting in to text messages is not a condition of purchasing or receiving any service. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We do not sell or share your information. ATX K9 does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. No mobile information — including your phone number and SMS opt-in consent — is shared with or sold to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

How we share information (limited)

We only share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (for example, our scheduling and messaging platform), and only so they can perform services for us. These providers are bound to protect your information and may not use it for their own purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law. As stated above, text-messaging opt-in data is never shared with third parties for marketing.

Cookies & tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (including Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our website and to improve our services. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work properly if you do.

Data retention & security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your choices

  • Opt out of texts anytime by replying STOP.
  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any message.
  • Request access to or deletion of your information by contacting us below.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent changes.
