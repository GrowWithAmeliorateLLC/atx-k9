New Puppy in Austin?

The first months matter most.

A new puppy is pure joy, and a whole lot of chewing, potty accidents, and sharp little teeth. What you do in those first weeks shapes the dog they become, so it pays to start right.

We focus on puppies under 16 weeks (about 4 months). For pups under 16 weeks (before their rabies shot), we come to you with in-home puppy lessons, building the foundation gently, then map out a clear path into our programs as your pup grows.

Why Start Young

Early training is easier training

The window is short. A puppy's prime socialization window closes fast. What they learn now is far easier than fixing habits later.

Prevent, don't repair. Heading off biting, jumping, and leash-pulling early means you never have to undo them.

A bond from the start. Gentle, positive training builds trust between you and your puppy from the very first week home.

Puppy Training FAQ

New-puppy questions, answered

How young can my puppy start?
As soon as they're home. For puppies under 16 weeks (before the rabies shot), we offer in-home puppy lessons so we can start the foundation safely and early. After 16 weeks, your pup is eligible for any of our programs.
Do you come to my home?
Yes, for under-16-week puppies our lessons are in-home, so your puppy learns right where they're growing up and the whole family can take part.
What can we actually fix at this age?
Plenty, potty and crate training, biting and nipping, early socialization, and a calm leash introduction, plus a gentle foundation of focus and good habits. We tailor the exact plan to your puppy on your free consultation. Starting now prevents the bigger problems later.
What methods do you use on a puppy?
Gentle, balanced, and age-appropriate. We make training fun and clear, with short puppy-sized sessions and the right tools for a young dog, never harsh for a pup still learning the world.
What happens after puppyhood?
When your pup hits 16 weeks, we map out the next step, private lessons if you want to stay hands-on, or board & train for faster, immersion-style results. You'll always have a clear path forward.
Does my puppy need to be spayed or neutered?
No, it's not required. Just keep us posted on your puppy's vaccination schedule so we plan socialization safely.
